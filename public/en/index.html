FAQ

How is this different from using ChatGPT or Claude?

It handles organizing your career, building a strategy for each company, laying it out in a fixed template and fitting the page count, checking numbers, and the rirekisho and interview prep, all in one flow. No dozens of back-and-forth messages or manual formatting.

How is what I enter handled?

It is sent to an AI service (Claude by Anthropic, US) to create your documents. What you send is not used to train AI. You can delete saved data at any time, and it is deleted automatically 1 year after your last use. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Can I submit the documents as they are?

You can save them as PDF and Word, formatted ready to submit. Before submitting, always check yourself that the content is factually accurate, especially flagged numbers and 【要記入】 spots.

Can I use it if my Japanese isn't strong?

Yes. You can enter your career in English or Chinese, and the documents are written in Japanese for Japanese companies. Each part of the documents comes with a translation in your language and notes on how it is written, and unnatural Japanese is pointed out. Because interviews are often held in Japanese, the motivation statement and sample answers are written in Japanese, with a translation. Before submitting, check yourself that the content is factually accurate.
